Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-12

This Privacy Policy describes how JAG Platform ("we," "us," "our"), operated by Robert Johnson-Attin, collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the JAG Platform application (jagcorporate.com) and its use of the WhatsApp Business Platform to communicate with prospective and current tenants, business contacts, and clients.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including but not limited to: name, phone number, email address, and messages exchanged through WhatsApp or our web application. If you submit a rental application, we also collect information relevant to that application, such as employment details, references, and identification documents you choose to upload.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to property enquiries, schedule viewings, and process rental applications
  • To send transactional messages via WhatsApp (e.g. viewing confirmations, application updates, rent reminders) to users who have engaged with us
  • To manage tenancy agreements and related business records
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

WhatsApp Messaging

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform to send and receive messages related to property enquiries, viewings, applications, and tenancy administration. Message content is stored securely and used only for the business purpose for which it was sent. We do not use WhatsApp messaging for unsolicited marketing to individuals who have not engaged with us.

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on infrastructure we operate and control directly. We do not sell personal information to third parties. Access to stored data is restricted to authorized personnel involved in property management operations.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.
